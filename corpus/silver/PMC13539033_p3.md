Eligible participants were children, adolescents, or adults with Tourette syndrome; the intervention was ecopipam; the comparator was placebo; and only randomized controlled trials (RCTs) were included.

Literature search strategy

A comprehensive literature search was conducted in PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, and ClinicalTrials.gov from database inception to 31 May 2026, without restrictions on publication date. Additional potentially eligible studies were identified through manual screening of the reference lists of included articles, relevant systematic reviews, conference proceedings, and clinical trial records. The search terms included combinations of “Tourette syndrome,” “Tourette disorder,” “tic disorder,” “ecopipam,” “SCH39166,” “dopamine D1 receptor antagonist,” “randomized,” “placebo,” and “clinical trial.” The search strategy was adapted for each database. The complete database-specific search strategies, including the date searched and the number of records retrieved, are provided in Supplementary Table S1.
