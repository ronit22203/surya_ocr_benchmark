Eligible study designs included RCTs and secondary analyses of randomized trial populations, including subgroup
analyses and post-trial or long-term follow-up reports. Studies were excluded if they involved premenopausal women,
did not provide findings relevant to older postmenopausal women, included populations outside the review scope,
evaluated interventions other than HRT, or did not report outcomes of interest. Observational designs, including
prospective cohort studies, retrospective cohort studies, case-control studies, cross-sectional studies, and registry-based
analyses, were excluded because HRT use in these studies may be influenced by baseline characteristics, indication for
treatment, prescribing patterns, and other factors that could introduce confounding bias and bias the final estimates. Although
the exclusion of observational studies narrowed the review to evidence arising from randomized exposure, it also
prevented the review from representing the full contemporary evidence base. Additionally, reviews, editorials, commen-
taries, letters, protocols, preclinical studies, and conference abstracts without sufficient data were excluded. No restric-
tions were placed on geographic location or clinical setting, but only English-language studies were included.

Information Sources and Search Strategy
A systematic search was conducted in PubMed, Embase, Scopus, and the Cochrane Library, including the
Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL), to identify the eligible literature. The search covered studies
published from January 1, 2021, through February 28, 2026. Search strategies combined database-specific controlled
vocabulary and free-text terms related to menopausal hormone therapy, including estrogen-only therapy, combined
estrogen–progestogen therapy, estrogen replacement therapy, postmenopausal hormone therapy, systemic estrogen
therapy, transdermal estradiol, and tibolone, together with terms for menopause and for cardiovascular, metabolic, and
renal outcomes. Where available, filters for publication year, language, sex, and age were applied at the database level;
otherwise, eligibility criteria were assessed during manual screening. Full search strategies for all databases are provided
in the Supplementary Material and Table S1.

Data Collection Process and Data Items
Data extraction was performed independently by two reviewers (TM and MFH) using a standardized data extraction
form. Disagreements were resolved through discussion and consensus.

The extracted data included study characteristics such as author, publication year, study design, trial name when
applicable, country or setting, and duration of follow-up; participant characteristics, including sample size, age, and
menopausal status; intervention and comparator details, including HRT regimen, formulation, dose, route of administra-
tion, duration of treatment, and comparator type; and reported cardiovascular, renal, and metabolic outcomes, including
outcome definitions, effect estimates, and measures of variability when available.
