However, the specific reasons for these exclusions—whether due to infection severity, glycemic control, vascular disease, or other factors—were not reported. This information is critical for assessing the external validity of the trial and understanding to which patient populations the results can be generalized [9, 10].

We acknowledge the authors' contribution to this important field and the transparency with which baseline data were presented. Nevertheless, these methodological considerations call for careful attention when interpreting the study’s conclusions. We would appreciate the authors' answer to these concerns.
